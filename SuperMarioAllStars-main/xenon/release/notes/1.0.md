The first release of Super Mario All-Stars for Xbox 360: the PC port by Darhkfox (v1.1.1), running natively on an
RGH/JTAG console as a homebrew `.xex`.

**You need your own *Super Mario All-Stars (USA)* ROM** (`.sfc`, 2 MiB, CRC32 `925637C7`). This release contains no
game data.

## What's in it

- The launcher, the four games (Super Mario Bros., The Lost Levels, Super Mario Bros. 2, Super Mario Bros. 3) and
  the All-Stars menu, with battery saves.
- The whole pause menu: save states, Level Select, Cheats, Sound Select, Music Player, Options and Credits.
- True widescreen, colour filters and palettes, and two-player co-op in SMB1, The Lost Levels and SMB3.
- Rewind and fast forward, and the game's 82 achievements with their in-game banner.
- Up to four controllers, 720p output, 48 kHz sound.

## Performance

- 4:3: 60 fps in the four games.
- Widescreen: 60 fps in SMB1, The Lost Levels, SMB2 and SMB3. SMB1 co-op in widescreen is at the limit and may drop a
  frame now and then. SMB3 co-op and the SMB3 battle game in widescreen are the least smooth; play them in 4:3 for a
  steady 60.

## Installing

1. Make a folder on the console's drive or on a USB drive and copy `SuperMarioAllStars.xex` into it.
2. Copy your ROM into the same folder, or into a `roms` folder inside it. Any file name works.
3. Launch `SuperMarioAllStars.xex` from Aurora, FSD or another homebrew dashboard.

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Pause menu | Back |
| Save / load state | Back + RB / Back + LB |
| Rewind / fast forward | LT / RT |
| 4:3 ↔ widescreen | Click the right stick |
| Pause | Guide button |
| Quit to the dashboard | Hold Back + LB + RB for 3 s, or Quit in the launcher |

## Known limitations

- Save states are not compatible with the PC version; battery saves and settings are.
- The *Super Mario All-Stars + Super Mario World* ROM is not supported.
- No native Xbox 360 achievements yet.
- No keyboard, file picker or network features.
